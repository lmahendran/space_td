Welcome to Space Tower Defense! This is your occasional round message. Start by placing your first tower somewhere, by clicking one on a tower in the sidebar, then clicking again where you want your tower. Oh, and if you want to get rid of this, just click on the message and it will go away.
As you saw the last round, a bunch of red balloons spawned. On this round, a some blue balloons will spawn. These contain each one red balloon, as well as its own layer. So, 2 layers to pop overall.
You can upgrade towers by selecting them, then buy the upgrade in that menu if you have enough money.
Time for a new balloon: the green balloon. This one holds a blue balloon, and has another layer. You can probably see a pattern here...
In this game, there isn't really a way to win. The goal is to make it as far as you can, with whatever method you can manage to put together.
You might be wondering what the five little squares are in the upgrade menu. The first one corresponds to a speed buff, second one range buff, third one discount, fourth one attack buff, and fifth one hidden vision. To be active, they will be coloured in.
Here comes the yellow balloons! One layer up from green, and moves even faster!
Something important to note is that the balloons get incrementally faster every round. Just good advice for later on.
Remember that your game gets saved at the end of every round! And if you ever need to, click the bottom left button mid-round if you ever want to pause and take a break.
The fastest of the coloured balloons are the pink balloons. One layer above yellow.
This is the last round of consistent messages. Messages will appear periodically if something new pops up. Make sure to come up with some strategy with the towers. You can learn about the towers in the menu. Good luck, and have fun!

Next balloon type: the rainbow balloon. This type contains 4 pink balloons inside of it.


The final balloon type for a while is the metal balloon. This balloon contains two rainbow balloons inside.



Here, you will see the first balloon effect: hidden balloons. These balloons are denoted with a camo cover and can only be seen by towers that have the green bar.




Get ready for regrow balloons! This is another effect that allows balloons to grow back to their original form. For added fun, coloured balloons will always regrow to a pink one, no matter where they start.




Have seven camo metals! It's an easy break if you have hidden defense, and an annoyance if you don't!
You've seen hidden, and you've seen regrow. How about hidden regrow?



A bonus tip: Boosters are great. At a certain point, they provide hidden vision to towers in range, which can take care of all of your hidden problems.




Time for your first boss balloon! These guys hold 3 metal balloons, and are a little strong. They become much stronger as rounds progress.
