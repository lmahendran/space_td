<html>Codes are a way to save your game progress on the computer, even after you're done playing. Saves are stored in the <u>.space_td\saves</u> folder inside your user folder; copy it to transfer your games to a different computer. Codes are generated automatically upon starting a new game, and your progress is saved every round. Make sure that you save your code once you stop playing, so that you can continue in the future!</html>
